I'm a first-year MS student at [Information Technology University (ITU)](https://itu.edu.pk/), where I work as a Research Intern at the [Intelligence Machine Lab](https://im.itu.edu.pk/) under [Dr. Waqas Sultani](https://waqassultani.github.io/), focusing on **3D Vision & Reconstruction** and **Image Generation**.

Before ITU, I completed my Bachelor's in Software Engineering at [Lahore Garrison University](https://lgu.edu.pk/), where I worked with [Dr. Rafaqat Alam Khan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=spYkiD8AAAAJ&hl=en) on computer vision for panoramic X-rays.

My undergraduate thesis focused on **Dental Enumeration and Disease Detection on Panoramic X-Rays** using deep learning. I'm actively exploring PhD programs and research collaboration opportunities.

**Research interests:** 3D Vision & Reconstruction, Image Generation, Computer Vision, Medical Image Processing, Large/Small Language Models, AI in Healthcare.
